Game modes

Five worlds. One forge.

Every mode starts at the hub, a floating forge island with walk-in portals to each world. Pick one with the compass or just walk through a gate.

/server skyblock

Skyblock

Start with a scrap of land in the void and build it into an empire. Climb island levels, finish challenges and fight for a spot in the Hall of Fame.

  • Island levels, challenges and player warps
  • Upgradable spawners paid in Mob Essence
  • Copper-golem robots that farm, mine and grind for you
NEW

OneBlock

One block under your feet. Break it and it comes back as something new. Work through the phases from grass to the End and grow a whole island from it.

  • Phase-based progression with rare drops
  • Miner robots keep breaking your block for you
  • Top OneBlock leaderboard with /istop
/server survival

Survival

Classic survival with a twist: a floating spawn town, chunk claims, an auction house and a 10,000-block world to explore.

  • KOTH every two hours
  • The Scrapyard PvP zone with supply drops
  • Crates, vouchers and custom enchants
/server factions

Factions

Build a faction, claim land and raid your rivals. PvP is always on, so pick your allies carefully and don't let anyone near your base.

  • Faction claims, power and alliances
  • Always-on PvP
  • Network-wide money and ranks
/server arcade

Arcade

Quick rounds when you just want to play. Jump into a queue and you're in a match in seconds.

  • Spleef, TNT Run and Infected
  • 2–16 players with automatic queues
  • Instant restarts, no waiting around
Survival PvP

Welcome to the Scrapyard

A 200×200 walled wasteland where anything goes. Every five hours around fifteen supply crates crash into the arena, loaded with upgraded gear, golden apples, potions and the occasional rare key. Getting in is easy: jump down a Drop Zone shaft. Getting out alive with the loot is the hard part.

~15 supply crates per drop 5h drop cycle 10s escape warm-up
Events

King of the Hill

Every two hours the KOTH arena opens. Hold the hill, outlast everyone else and claim the KOTH crate, which has a small chance of dropping a rank voucher.

Every 2h on Survival +200 Scrap for the winner KOTH crate key
Built in-house

More than plugins

Most of BlackForge runs on our own custom plugin, so the systems are made for this network and work together.

Custom enchants

Lifesteal, Thunderstrike, Bleed, Overload, Springs, Autosmelt and Excavator. Drag a book onto your gear to apply it.

Robots

Copper-golem workers that farm, mine, grind and fish while fuelled. Upgrade them from tier I to V.

Spawner upgrades

Upgrade spawn speed, mob count, range and Essence yield, or change the mob type. Upgrades stay on the spawner when you Silk Touch it.

Crates & keys

Iron, Gold, Netherite, KOTH and Vote crates. Win keys from events, voting, supply drops and play time.

Auction house

List anything with /ah and trade with the whole server. Your money is the same on every mode.

Shared economy

Money and Scrap travel with you across every server. Spend Scrap in the Scrap Shop on spawners, tags and more.

Fair play

GrimAC plus our own Guardian system catches fly, reach, x-ray and auto-clickers, and the chat filter stops slurs and spam.

Leaderboards

Richest players, top islands and top OneBlocks, shown on live boards in the Hall of Fame.

How to join

Play in a minute

BlackForge is a Java Edition server. Any version from 1.13 up to the latest release works.

  1. Open MinecraftLaunch Minecraft: Java Edition and click Multiplayer.
  2. Add the serverClick Add Server and enter blackforgemc.com
  3. Pick your worldJoin, then walk through a portal at the hub or use the compass.
FAQ

Good questions

Which Minecraft versions can join?

Java Edition 1.13 up to 26.3. The latest version is recommended so you see every block and feature as intended.

Can I play on Bedrock (console, mobile, Windows 10/11)?

Not yet. BlackForge is Java Edition only for now. Bedrock support may come later; watch the Discord for news.

Is my progress shared between game modes?

Your money, Scrap and rank are shared across every server. Items, islands and claims stay in the mode where you earned them.

Is the server free to play?

Yes. Everything that affects gameplay is earned by playing. The store only sells cosmetics, like supporter badges, particle trails and name colours.

I got muted or banned. What now?

Read the server rules to see what happened. If you think it was a mistake, open a ticket in our Discord and a staff member will review it.

How do I report a hacker or rule-breaker?

Our anti-cheat catches most cheats automatically. For anything else, take a screenshot or clip and report it in the Discord.
